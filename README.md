# US-COVID-19-Disparities-Factors-and-Insights-at-the-County-Level

# Project Description
This project undertakes a comprehensive analysis of the uneven spread of COVID-19 across US counties between 2020 and 2022. It investigates how various demographic, behavioral, and healthcare capacity factors contributed to the observed disparities in COVID-19 case rates. By examining state differences, age profiles, education levels, grocery mobility patterns, and the availability of patient-care physicians, this research aims to answer why some communities were disproportionately affected by the pandemic. The findings provide critical insights for public health preparedness, policy design, and the effective allocation of medical resources in future health crises.

#Key Research Questions
• How do grocery mobility patterns relate to variations in COVID-19 cases across US counties?

• What is the impact of age structure (specifically, the proportion of residents aged 65 and older) on COVID-19 transmission dynamics at the county level?

• How do education levels (e.g., percentage of population with less than a high school education) correlate with COVID-19 case rates?

• What role does healthcare capacity, such as the availability of active patient-care physicians and ICU beds, play in the uneven spread of COVID-19?

# Data Sources
This analysis combines county-level COVID-19 case data from the Johns Hopkins University Coronavirus Resource Centre with publicly available Google mobility data and U.S. county demographic and healthcare statistics.

3. Install R Packages: Open the GP3-Group4.Rmd file in RStudio and install any missing packages listed in the setup chunk (e.g., tidyverse, lubridate, scales, etc.) using install.packages("package_name").
