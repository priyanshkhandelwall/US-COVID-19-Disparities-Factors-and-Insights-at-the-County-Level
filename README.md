# 🦠 US-COVID-19-Disparities: Factors and Insights at the County Level

## 📌 Project Description
This project presents a comprehensive analysis of the uneven spread of COVID-19 across U.S. counties between 2020 and 2022. It investigates how demographic, behavioral, and healthcare capacity factors contributed to disparities in COVID-19 case rates.

By examining state-level differences, age profiles, education levels, grocery mobility patterns, and the availability of patient-care physicians, this analysis aims to explain why certain communities were disproportionately affected. The findings provide actionable insights for public health preparedness, policy design, and effective allocation of medical resources in future health crises.

---

## ❓ Key Research Questions
- 🛒 How do grocery mobility patterns relate to variations in COVID-19 cases across U.S. counties?
- 👴 What is the impact of age structure (specifically the proportion of residents aged 65 and older) on COVID-19 transmission dynamics at the county level?
- 🎓 How do education levels (e.g., percentage of population with less than a high school education) correlate with COVID-19 case rates?
- 🏥 What role does healthcare capacity, such as the availability of active patient-care physicians and ICU beds, play in the uneven spread of COVID-19?

---

## 📊 Data Sources
This analysis combines:
- County-level COVID-19 case data from the **Johns Hopkins University Coronavirus Resource Centre**
- Publicly available **Google Mobility data**
- U.S. county-level **demographic and healthcare statistics**
